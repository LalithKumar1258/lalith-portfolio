SHIPPED · IN USE

AskCM — data answers in minutes, not days.

A multi-agent analytics platform for business teams at CreditMantri.

Business teams needed numbers — and the why behind them. Getting them meant queuing on the data team: up to ~2 days of waiting, and a typical request took about two hours.

Give teams trusted answers in minutes — without giving an AI unrestricted access to the database or ever exposing credentials.

I evolved AskCM from a RAG text-to-SQL bot into a customised LibreChat deployment with my own MCP server over ClickHouse — eight approved tools (glossary search, describe_table, run_query, spend tracking…). Three specialist agents — CFU, Referrals, ARS — mirror the data team's business SPOCs. ~12 large tables were pre-joined into 6 golden tables. Guardrails: read-only, table allowlist, audit log. Cost: model switching, prompt reduction, per-user budgets.

A typical request went from ~2 hours to under 10 minutes; cost per question from ₹2.75 to ₹0.44 (~85% lower). All 39/39 adversarial test queries refused, and a cross-check matched 453,702 logged-in users exactly.
